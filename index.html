Merchant Operations Management

Mobile web app for staff: daily tasks, merchant list, business inspections with checklist, notes, GPS and photo evidence. Reports go to management on Telegram.

Open the app
  • Sign in with your Staff ID and PIN
  • Record inspections with photos and location
  • Management is alerted automatically about failed or high-risk inspections
Tip: on your phone, open the app and choose "Add to Home screen".
